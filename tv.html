bilal

Put it on a screen in your house.

Bilal is built for a screen you walk past, not a phone in your pocket. It calls you at the moment you need to leave for jama'ah, not when the prayer has already started.

bilalathan.co.uk

How it goes

  1. Open the site on the screen. It shows a QR code. (There's a short code underneath if your camera won't focus, or if the screen has redrawn it.)
  2. Scan that code with this phone. Pick your mosque and how far you walk. The screen picks it up on its own.
  3. One press for sound. The screen reloads itself and asks once — after that it can call you to jama'ah for as long as it stays on.

Same three moments on every device. The cards below are only about getting a browser open.

Pick your screen

Amazon Echo Showeasiest

  1. Say "Alexa, open Silk browser"
  2. Go to bilalathan.co.uk, then follow the three steps above

Nothing else to configure, and the Show is a touchscreen so the sound press is just a tap. Echo Shows normally close the browser after about ten minutes — the site keeps itself awake, so it stays put.

Fire TV Stick

  1. Open the Silk browser. If it isn't installed, search Silk in Find — it's free
  2. Go to bilalathan.co.uk, then follow the three steps above
  3. Settings → Display & Sounds → Screensaver → Never

Be warned: Fire TV also has a separate sleep timer, around 14 minutes, that Amazon hide completely — turning the screensaver off does not stop it. There is no setting for it. A proper Fire TV app is the real fix and it's the next thing being built.

A spare phone or tablet

  1. Open bilalathan.co.uk/?display=1 — the ?display=1 matters, it tells a phone to show the screen rather than this page
  2. Scan the code it shows with your main phone, then follow the steps above
  3. Leave it on charge where you'll walk past it

The cheapest way to cover a second room. An old handset on a kitchen shelf works, and each screen calls its own athan.

A smart TV with a browser

  1. Open the TV's built-in browser — most Samsung and LG sets have one
  2. Go to bilalathan.co.uk, then follow the three steps above

Whether it stays on depends entirely on the make. Worth ten minutes of testing before you rely on it.
