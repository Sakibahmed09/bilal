bilal

Put it on a screen in your house.

Bilal is built for a screen you walk past, not a phone in your pocket. It calls you at the moment you need to leave for jama'ah, not when the prayer has already started.

bilalathan.co.uk

How it goes

  1. Open the site on the screen. It shows a QR code. (There's a short code underneath if your camera won't focus, or if the screen has redrawn it.)
  2. Scan that code with this phone. Pick your mosque and how far you walk. The screen picks it up on its own.
  3. One press for sound. The screen reloads itself and asks once. After that it can call you to jama'ah for as long as it stays on. (The TV app skips this — it arms itself.)

Same three moments whatever screen you use. All that changes below is how you get Bilal open on it.

The one that just works

Amazon Echo Show

  1. Say "Alexa, open Silk browser"
  2. Go to bilalathan.co.uk, then follow the three steps above

Nothing to configure and nothing to work around. It's a touchscreen, so the sound step is just a tap. Echo Shows normally close the browser after about ten minutes, but the site keeps itself awake, so it stays put.

On a TV

Fire TV Stick & Android TVThe app

  1. Get Downloader (free, orange icon) from your TV's app store and open it. If the TV asks, flip the switch that lets Downloader install apps — it shows you where
  2. Type bilalathan.co.uk/bilal.apk into Downloader, install Bilal, open it
  3. Follow the phone steps above — no press for sound needed

This is the proper answer on a TV. The screen stays on without any settings, the athan arms itself, and after a power cut it starts itself back up. The catch: the two newest sticks — Fire TV Stick 4K Select and the 2026 Fire TV Stick HD — run Vega OS and can't install apps like this at all. For those, use the Silk browser below.

No app? Three other ways

All three work. Each one asks something of you first, which is the only reason the Show and the app are above them.

Fire TV Stick — Silk browser

  1. Open the Silk browser. If it isn't installed, search Silk in Find. It's free
  2. Go to bilalathan.co.uk, then follow the three steps above
  3. Settings → Display & Sounds → Screensaver → Never

The catch: Fire TV has a second sleep timer, around 14 minutes, that Amazon hide completely. Turning the screensaver off does not stop it, so the screen goes dark on its own. The app above doesn't have this problem — the browser route is for the Vega OS sticks that can't install it.

A spare phone or tablet

  1. Open bilalathan.co.uk/?display=1 (the ?display=1 tells a phone to show the screen rather than this page)
  2. Scan the code it shows with your main phone, then follow the steps above
  3. Leave it on charge where you'll walk past it

The catch: you have to remember that parameter, or the phone shows you this page again. Otherwise it's the cheapest way to cover a second room, and each screen calls its own athan.

A smart TV with a browser

  1. Open the TV's built-in browser. Most Samsung and LG sets have one
  2. Go to bilalathan.co.uk, then follow the three steps above

The catch: whether it stays on depends entirely on the make, and we can't test every set. Give it ten minutes before you rely on it.
